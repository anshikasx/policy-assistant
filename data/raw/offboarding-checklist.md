# Offboarding Checklist

Effective: 1 January 2026
Applies to: Employees leaving Nimbus Technologies

## Notice and Coordination

When an employee’s departure is confirmed, HR coordinates the offboarding process with the employee’s manager, IT, Finance, and other relevant teams.

The employee’s final working date must be clearly recorded.

## Manager Responsibilities

The manager should identify:

* Outstanding work
* Important deadlines
* Active projects
* Knowledge-transfer requirements
* Customer or stakeholder responsibilities
* Documents and repositories requiring ownership transfer

## IT Access

IT disables or removes access according to the employee’s final working date and security requirements.

Company accounts, VPN access, internal applications, and other system access may be disabled as part of the process.

Employees must not attempt to retain access after their employment ends.

## Company Property

Employees must return company-owned laptops, accessories, ID cards, storage devices, and other equipment.

IT records returned equipment and confirms its condition.

## Data and Documents

Employees must transfer business documents and work-related information to the appropriate company location or manager.

Company information must not be copied to personal accounts, devices, or storage services.

## Finance and HR

Finance processes eligible final payments and approved outstanding reimbursements according to applicable company procedures.

HR conducts the required exit process and provides information about benefits and other employment matters.

## Final Day

Before leaving, employees should complete knowledge transfer, return company property, resolve outstanding expense claims, and complete required HR documentation.
