# Parental Leave Policy

Effective: 1 January 2026
Applies to: Eligible full-time employees, Nimbus Technologies

## Purpose

Nimbus Technologies provides parental leave to eligible employees following the birth, adoption, or placement of a child, subject to applicable law and company eligibility requirements.

## Eligibility

Employees should contact HR to confirm their eligibility, available leave duration, documentation requirements, and applicable statutory provisions.

Eligibility may vary based on the employee’s location and employment circumstances.

## Leave Request

Employees should notify their manager and HR as early as reasonably possible when planning parental leave.

The request should include the expected start date and anticipated duration.

HR may request appropriate documentation supporting the leave request.

## During Leave

Employees on approved parental leave are not expected to perform normal work duties during their approved leave period.

Employees should coordinate important work handovers with their manager before beginning leave where reasonably possible.

## Benefits

The treatment of salary, benefits, insurance, and other employment benefits during parental leave is determined according to applicable law and Nimbus benefits rules for the employee’s location.

## Return to Work

Employees should coordinate their return date with HR and their manager.

Nimbus will make reasonable efforts to support a smooth return to work and may discuss flexible arrangements where business and legal requirements permit.

## Confidentiality

Information relating to parental leave should be handled confidentially by managers, HR, and other authorized personnel.

Employees with questions about eligibility or documentation should contact HR rather than relying on informal guidance from colleagues.
