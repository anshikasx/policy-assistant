# Security Incident Reporting Policy

Effective: 1 January 2026
Applies to: All employees, contractors, and authorized users of Nimbus Technologies systems

## Purpose

Security incidents must be reported quickly so that Nimbus Information Security can investigate, contain, and reduce potential damage.

## Reportable Incidents

Employees must report suspected incidents including:

* Lost or stolen company devices
* Suspected account compromise
* Unexpected MFA prompts
* Phishing or suspicious emails
* Accidental disclosure of confidential information
* Malware or suspicious software
* Unauthorized access
* Unusual system activity
* Misuse of company credentials

## Reporting

Security incidents should be reported through the Nimbus Security Incident Portal.

If the portal is unavailable, employees should contact the Nimbus IT Service Desk and clearly state that the issue may be security-related.

Urgent incidents should be reported immediately rather than waiting for additional information.

## Do Not Investigate Alone

Employees should not attempt to independently investigate, delete evidence, contact suspected attackers, or reset affected systems unless instructed by Information Security or IT.

When safe to do so, employees should preserve relevant emails, messages, screenshots, filenames, and other evidence.

## Lost Devices

A lost or stolen company laptop or mobile device must be reported immediately.

IT may remotely lock or erase company-managed equipment when necessary.

## No-Blame Reporting

Employees are encouraged to report mistakes and suspected incidents promptly.

Reporting an accidental security mistake quickly helps Nimbus reduce its impact.

Employees must not delay reporting because they are uncertain whether an event qualifies as a security incident.
