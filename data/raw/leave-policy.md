# Leave Policy

Effective: 1 January 2026
Applies to: All full-time employees, Nimbus Technologies

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees accrue 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year,
credited at 2 days per month. Leave accrued but unused may be carried
forward up to a maximum of 12 days into the following year. Any balance
beyond 12 days lapses on 31 December.

Employees in their probation period (first 90 days) accrue leave but may
not use it except with written approval from their reporting manager.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave does not
carry forward. Absences of three or more consecutive days require a medical
certificate submitted to HR within five working days of returning.

## Applying for Leave

All leave requests are submitted through the Nimbus HR Portal. Requests for
more than three consecutive days must be submitted at least 14 days in
advance. Manager approval is required for all leave.

## Leave Encashment

Employees may encash up to 10 days of unused annual leave at the end of the
financial year, calculated at basic salary rate.
