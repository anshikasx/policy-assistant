# Internet Usage Policy

Effective: 1 January 2026
Applies to: All employees using Nimbus Technologies networks or company-managed devices

## Purpose

Nimbus Technologies provides internet access to support business operations, communication, research, and collaboration.

Internet usage must comply with company policies, security requirements, and applicable law.

## Acceptable Use

Employees may use company internet access for legitimate business activities.

Limited personal browsing is permitted when it does not interfere with work, consume excessive company resources, or create security or compliance risks.

## Prohibited Activities

Employees must not use Nimbus systems to:

* Access illegal content
* Conduct unauthorized hacking or security testing
* Distribute malware
* Circumvent security controls
* Download pirated software or copyrighted material without authorization
* Operate unauthorized commercial activities
* Access or distribute abusive or threatening content

## Downloads

Employees should download software and files only from trusted sources.

Software installations must comply with the Software Procurement Policy and IT requirements.

Suspicious downloads should be reported to IT or Information Security.

## Network Security

Employees must not attempt to bypass network filtering, VPN controls, endpoint protection, or other security mechanisms.

Personal hotspots may be used when necessary for business continuity, but company security requirements still apply.

## Monitoring

Nimbus may monitor network and system activity to maintain security, investigate incidents, troubleshoot systems, and comply with legal or regulatory requirements, subject to applicable law and company policy.

## Privacy

Employees should not assume that activity conducted on company-managed systems or networks is private.

Personal accounts should not be used to store or transmit Nimbus confidential information.
