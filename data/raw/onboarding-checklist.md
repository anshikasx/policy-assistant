# Onboarding Checklist

Effective: 1 January 2026
Applies to: New employees joining Nimbus Technologies

## Before Joining

HR creates the employee record and confirms the joining date, department, reporting manager, and assigned office.

IT prepares required accounts and equipment based on the employee’s role.

## First Day

New employees should complete the following:

* Meet their reporting manager
* Complete HR documentation
* Collect or confirm company laptop assignment
* Activate Nimbus account
* Configure MFA
* Review security policies
* Review employee handbook
* Confirm team communication channels

## First Week

During the first week, employees should:

* Complete mandatory security training
* Complete required compliance training
* Meet key team members
* Understand their role and responsibilities
* Review relevant department processes
* Confirm access to required business applications
* Set initial goals with their manager

## First 30 Days

Managers should ensure that new employees understand team objectives, performance expectations, and major workflows.

Employees should complete any role-specific technical or operational training.

## Access

Access to systems is granted according to job responsibilities and least-privilege principles.

Employees should report missing or excessive access through the IT Service Portal.

## Completion

HR and the employee’s manager are responsible for ensuring required onboarding activities are completed.

Mandatory security and compliance training should be completed within the deadlines communicated by HR.

The onboarding process is considered complete when required HR, IT, security, and role-specific activities have been completed.
