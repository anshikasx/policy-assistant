# Employee Referral Program

Effective: 1 January 2026
Applies to: All eligible Nimbus Technologies employees

## Purpose

The employee referral program allows Nimbus employees to recommend qualified candidates for open positions.

Referrals help Nimbus identify candidates who may be a strong fit for the company’s roles and culture.

## Making a Referral

Employees may submit candidates through the Nimbus Careers Referral Portal.

A referral should include the candidate’s current contact information, resume, and the role for which the employee believes the candidate is suitable.

Employees should obtain the candidate’s permission before submitting personal information.

## Eligibility

Regular full-time employees may participate unless excluded by their role or applicable HR rules.

Employees directly involved in recruiting decisions for a particular position may not be eligible for referral rewards for that position.

## Referral Bonus

Eligible employees may receive a referral bonus when their referred candidate is hired and completes the required eligibility period.

The applicable bonus amount depends on the role and referral program terms in effect at the time of referral.

Finance and HR determine the final payment eligibility.

## Multiple Referrals

If multiple employees refer the same candidate, the referral recorded first in the official referral system is normally considered the valid referral.

Informal recommendations through email, chat, or personal conversations do not replace an official referral submission.

## Hiring Decisions

A referral does not guarantee an interview or employment.

All candidates are evaluated using Nimbus recruitment processes and applicable hiring standards.

Employees must not make promises about compensation, hiring outcomes, or employment terms to referred candidates.
