# Expense Reimbursement Policy

Effective: 1 January 2026
Applies to: All full-time employees, Nimbus Technologies

## Eligible Expenses

Nimbus Technologies reimburses reasonable business expenses incurred by employees while performing authorized company work.

Eligible expenses include approved business travel, client meetings, conference fees, local transportation, meals during business travel, and other expenses specifically approved by the employee’s manager.

Personal expenses are not reimbursable.

## Approval

Employees must obtain manager approval before committing to expenses expected to exceed INR 25,000 or the equivalent amount in another currency.

Expenses related to international travel require approval under the Travel Policy before booking.

## Submission

Employees must submit reimbursement claims through the Nimbus Expense Portal within 30 calendar days of the expense.

Each claim must include:

* Date of expense
* Business purpose
* Amount and currency
* Appropriate cost center
* Itemized receipt or invoice

Credit-card statements alone are not considered sufficient documentation when an itemized receipt is available.

## Processing

Finance reviews submitted claims for policy compliance. Approved claims are normally reimbursed through the employee’s regular payroll account.

Claims with missing documentation may be returned to the employee for correction.

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are generally not reimbursable:

* Personal entertainment
* Alcohol unless specifically approved for a client or company event
* Traffic or parking fines
* Personal purchases
* Expenses incurred by unauthorized companions
* Premium upgrades without prior approval

Employees must not split a single expense into multiple claims to avoid an approval threshold.
