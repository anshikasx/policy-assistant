# Travel Policy

Effective: 1 January 2026
Applies to: All full-time employees, Nimbus Technologies

## Business Travel

Business travel must have a legitimate business purpose and be approved by the employee’s reporting manager before booking.

International travel also requires approval from the relevant department head.

## Booking

Employees must book flights, hotels, and other major travel arrangements through the approved Nimbus travel platform whenever the required option is available.

Travel should normally be booked at least 14 days in advance.

## Air Travel

Economy-class airfare is the standard for domestic and international flights.

Employees may request premium economy for flights longer than eight hours with prior manager approval. Business-class travel requires executive-level approval.

## Accommodation

Employees should use hotels available through the approved travel platform and select reasonably priced business accommodation.

Hotel expenses include the room and applicable taxes. Personal room service, entertainment, minibar purchases, and other personal charges are not reimbursable.

## Ground Transportation

Reasonable taxi, rideshare, airport transfer, and public transportation costs incurred for business purposes are reimbursable.

Employees should choose economical transportation appropriate to the business requirement.

## Travel Advances

Employees may request a travel advance through Finance before an approved trip. Unused advances must be reconciled after returning.

## International Travel

Employees are responsible for ensuring that required passports, visas, and travel documents are valid. Nimbus may reimburse approved visa and business travel documentation costs.

Travelers must comply with local laws and Nimbus security requirements while abroad.
