# Work From Home Policy

Effective: 1 January 2026
Applies to: All full-time employees, Nimbus Technologies

## Eligibility

Nimbus Technologies supports hybrid work for eligible roles where business operations allow remote work. Employees must complete onboarding and receive approval from their reporting manager before regularly working from home.

Employees whose roles require regular physical access to company facilities, equipment, laboratories, or other controlled environments may have different attendance requirements.

## Hybrid Work

Eligible employees may work from home up to three days per week. Employees are expected to work from their assigned office on designated collaboration days established by their team.

Managers may require additional office attendance for team meetings, customer visits, training, or operational requirements.

## Working Hours

Employees working remotely must remain available during their team’s agreed working hours. The standard collaboration window is 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time.

Employees must maintain the same productivity, communication, and availability standards while working remotely as they would in the office.

## Work Environment

Employees are responsible for maintaining a safe and suitable workspace. Company laptops and approved equipment must be used according to Nimbus security policies.

Employees must not allow unauthorized individuals to access company systems or confidential information from their home workspace.

## Temporary Remote Work

Temporary work from another city or country requires prior written approval from the reporting manager and HR. International remote work may also require review by Finance, Legal, and Information Security.

Remote work is a work arrangement and does not change an employee’s official work location unless formally approved.
