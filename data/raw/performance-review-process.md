# Performance Review Process

Effective: 1 January 2026
Applies to: Full-time employees, Nimbus Technologies

## Purpose

Nimbus Technologies uses performance reviews to provide structured feedback, evaluate progress against goals, recognize contributions, and identify development opportunities.

## Review Cycle

The standard performance cycle runs annually.

Employees establish goals with their managers at the beginning of the cycle and review progress throughout the year.

Managers should provide ongoing feedback rather than waiting until the formal review.

## Goal Setting

Goals should be specific, measurable, relevant to the employee’s role, and aligned with team and company priorities.

Goals may cover business results, technical contributions, collaboration, leadership, customer impact, or other role-specific responsibilities.

## Mid-Year Check-In

Employees and managers complete a mid-year check-in to discuss progress, challenges, priorities, and any required changes to goals.

Goals may be adjusted when business priorities materially change.

## Annual Review

The annual review includes employee self-assessment and manager assessment.

Managers evaluate performance based on documented goals, outcomes, behaviors, and contributions during the review period.

## Ratings

Nimbus uses a standardized performance-rating framework communicated by HR before each review cycle.

Performance ratings should reflect the full review period rather than isolated events.

## Development

Managers and employees should identify development priorities and create practical plans for improving skills or preparing for future responsibilities.

Employees may discuss career interests and development opportunities with their managers or HR.

## Confidentiality

Performance information is confidential and should only be shared with authorized individuals involved in the review process.
