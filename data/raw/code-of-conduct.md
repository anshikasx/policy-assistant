# Code of Conduct

Effective: 1 January 2026
Applies to: All employees and representatives of Nimbus Technologies

## Purpose

Nimbus Technologies expects employees to act professionally, ethically, respectfully, and responsibly in all business activities.

## Respectful Workplace

Employees must treat colleagues, customers, partners, and other individuals with dignity and respect.

Harassment, bullying, discrimination, intimidation, threats, and abusive behavior are not acceptable.

## Integrity

Employees must provide accurate information in company records and communications.

Employees must not falsify expenses, records, performance information, credentials, or other business information.

## Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose actual or potential conflicts of interest to their manager or HR.

Employees should not allow personal interests to improperly influence business decisions.

## Confidentiality

Employees must protect Nimbus confidential information and use it only for authorized business purposes.

Confidential information must not be shared with unauthorized individuals or transferred to personal storage without approval.

## Company Resources

Company equipment, systems, funds, and information must be used responsibly and for legitimate purposes.

Employees must follow applicable security and technology policies.

## Gifts and Business Courtesies

Employees must avoid accepting or providing gifts or benefits that could improperly influence a business decision.

Questions about gifts, hospitality, or potential conflicts should be directed to HR or the appropriate compliance contact.

## Reporting Concerns

Employees who become aware of suspected misconduct should report it through the appropriate Nimbus reporting channel or to HR.

Retaliation against an employee who raises a good-faith concern is not permitted.

Violations of the Code of Conduct may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment.
