# Laptop and Hardware Policy

Effective: 1 January 2026
Applies to: All full-time employees, Nimbus Technologies

## Company Equipment

Nimbus Technologies provides employees with company-managed laptops and other equipment required for their roles.

Standard equipment is determined by job function and approved by the employee’s manager and IT.

## Acceptable Use

Company hardware must primarily be used for authorized business activities. Employees may use devices for limited personal activities provided that such use does not violate company policies or interfere with work.

Employees must not install unauthorized software, disable security controls, or modify company-managed configurations.

## Security

Employees must keep company equipment physically secure. Laptops must be locked whenever unattended.

Company laptops must use the security configuration managed by Nimbus IT, including encryption, endpoint protection, and required operating-system updates.

## Damage or Loss

Lost, stolen, or damaged company equipment must be reported to IT as soon as possible.

A stolen laptop should also be reported to local authorities where appropriate.

Employees must not attempt to repair company hardware themselves unless specifically authorized by IT.

## Replacement

Hardware replacement is based on business need, device condition, and IT assessment.

Employees should return defective equipment to IT rather than purchasing replacement equipment independently.

## Return of Equipment

All company hardware must be returned when an employee leaves Nimbus Technologies or when IT requests replacement.

Employees must not retain company storage devices, accessories, or equipment after their employment ends.

IT may remotely lock or erase company-managed devices when necessary to protect company information.
